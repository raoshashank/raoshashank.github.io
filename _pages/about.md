Hi! I am a Fourth year Ph.D. Student at the National University of Singapore, working with Dr. Harold Soh at the [Collaborative, Learning, and Adaptive Robotics (CLeAR)](https://clear-nus.github.io/).

**Research**: I'm generally interested in human-robot interaction, collaboration and Explainable-AI. Currently, I am focussed on the Synthesis and Evaluation of Social Navigation Algorithms that would aid robots to behave in a context-appropriate and socially-compliant manner around humans.


**Previously**: For my Masters Thesis at Arizona State University, I worked on Learning action models of black-box agents through agent-interrogation with [Dr. Siddharth Srivastava](https://aair-lab.github.io/) and before that, I worked on AI agents that generate explanations for planning tasks for humans with [Dr. Yu (Tony) Zhang](https://www.public.asu.edu/~yzhan442/).
Before that, I worked on Mapping Algorithms with Dr. [K.R. Guruprasad](https://www.iitk.ac.in/new/k-r-guruprasad) and building Robots from scratch for the [ABU-ROBOCON](https://robocon.nitk.ac.in/#) competition at the National Institute of Technology, Karnataka.

When I'm not working on research, you would generally find me either playing some [guitar](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCGAl4cALoCjJDZFncgKvKnw), or cooking, or sketching, or watching british panel/comedy shows.
Previously, I was the Social Secretary of the [NUS Graduate Student Society](https://www.nusgss.com/), where I organized social events to engage and invigorate NUS Graduate students.
